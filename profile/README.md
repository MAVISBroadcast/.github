Mavis builds powerful, mobile-first tools that transform iPhones, iPads, and Apple TVs into professional cameras, monitors, live streamers, camera-to-cloud capture devices, and live production systems.

At our core is a belief that the future of content creation is connected, mobile and cloud-enabled, empowering creatives to do more with less. The company is building an ecosystem of apps that work seamlessly together to deliver powerful, flexible, and accessible production workflows.
